# Defense Policy — Aodı Akdıro Shield OS

Adopt, sign, then activate into the **🛡️ Defense Policy** module checklist (the same 18 items rated high / med / low).

**Ground rule:** approval first, activation second; rejections documented with a reason.

## Identity and secrets (high risk)
1. Two-step verification mandatory — only you can waive it, in writing.
2. Least privilege: the role defines access, not the person.
3. All secrets and keys in one vault with an owner each.
4. No account or password sharing — even inside the leadership team.
5. Rotation of keys and service secrets on a written schedule.

## Devices and data (high risk)
6. Every device registered, protected and updated before touching work data.
7. Backups that survive ransomware, with a tested restore.
8. Full-disk encryption on portable devices — no exceptions.
9. Patch cycle with published deadlines per severity tier.
10. Vulnerability scans scheduled, results ranked by real exposure.
11. Annual external penetration test after major changes.

## Response and governance (high/med/low)
12. Written incident runbook: who contains, who reports, within what time.
13. Every incident documented with lessons, not blame.
14. Quarterly access review — leavers and expired contracts removed same day.
15. Monthly phishing simulation measured with numbers, training without blame.
16. Deny-by-default firewall; every open port has a reason and a number.
17. A security clause written into every vendor contract holding your data.
18. Quarterly policy review with signed changes.
